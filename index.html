Getting to Coral Rest Beach

A long curve of white sand just east of the Hip Strip, twelve minutes from the airport. Three stages, a food village under the almond trees and the Caribbean Sea doing what it does best.

By air

Fly into Sangster International. The beach is twelve minutes by taxi and our rideshare point sits right outside arrivals on all three days.

Festival shuttle

Free with every pass. Shuttles leave the Hip Strip every twenty minutes from 4 PM and run back until half an hour after the last set.

Driving

Parking is limited and must be booked ahead for JMD 3,500 a night. Taxis and drivers have their own drop lane at the north gate.

Questions people ask

Still wondering about something? Write to hello@basslinebay.example and a real person will reply within a day.

Gates open at 5 PM each night so you can catch the sunset. Music runs until 2 AM on Thursday and 4 AM on Friday and Saturday. Last entry is at midnight.

Single night General tickets go on sale on 1 March 2027 at JMD 10,500. VIP and Cabana are sold as full three night passes only.

Bassline Bay is for over 18s. Bring a valid government photo ID or passport. We cannot make exceptions at the gate, so please check before you travel.

The water is open until 8 PM each night, with lifeguards along the marked area in front of the Reef Stage. After dark the shoreline stays open but the sea does not.

The site is cashless. Load your wristband online or at top up points around the beach using any debit or credit card. Unused balance is refunded in the week after the festival.

Sunscreen, an empty refillable bottle, a small bag and comfortable sandals. Leave glass, umbrellas, speakers and drones at home.

Yes. Matting runs from the main gate to a raised viewing area at each stage, and a free companion ticket is available for anyone who needs one. Tell us what you need and we will plan it with you.
